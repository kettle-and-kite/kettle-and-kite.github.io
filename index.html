Kettle & Kite Pin Publisher

A private Pinterest publishing tool for the Kettle & Kite shop

Kettle & Kite is a small online shop that sells print-on-demand apparel and mugs on Etsy (kettleandkite.etsy.com). Kettle & Kite Pin Publisher is the shop’s own internal integration with Pinterest. The shop owner uses it to publish Kettle & Kite product Pins to the shop’s own Pinterest business account.

What it does

  • Publishes Pins of Kettle & Kite’s own products, each linking to the matching Kettle & Kite listing on Etsy. The shop owner approves every Pin individually before it is published.
  • Organizes those Pins on the shop’s own boards.
  • Reads Pinterest’s analytics for the Pins it published, so the shop can see how they perform.

How it connects to Pinterest

The shop owner signs in with Pinterest OAuth, through Pinterest’s own sign-in and consent screen. The integration uses Pinterest’s official API with the permissions described in the privacy policy. It never asks for or stores a Pinterest password.

Not a public service

Kettle & Kite Pin Publisher is not offered to the public. There is no sign-up, and it cannot be used with anyone else’s Pinterest account. It does not engage with other Pinterest users or their content.
